# 03 — Social Kit (captions, hooks, hashtags, posting plan)

> **Media lives outside this repository.** The finished MP4s, narration MP3s, scene plates and
> cover thumbnails are kept in the standalone *KidBus marketing pack* (git-ignored by design: they
> are ~90 MB of binaries). Paths such as `videos/`, `videos/9x16/`, `social/srt/`, `social/thumbs/`,
> `assets/ai/` and `tools/` in this folder refer to that pack. This folder is the source of truth for
> the *text*: scripts, prompts, captions and the client brief. Nothing here affects the product build.

Ready-to-post copy for all eleven films. Copy the block you need; nothing here repeats the video's own
on-screen words too literally — the caption's job is to earn the *click* and the *comment*.

**Accounts to create before posting (10 minutes):**

- Instagram business profile: **@kidbus** (or `@kidbus.by.zeromilesystems` if taken) — bio:
  `Real-time school bus tracking for Indian schools 🚌 Live GPS · Boarding alerts · SOS ·
  Free pilot ↓` + link to `zeromilesystems.com`
- LinkedIn page: **ZeroMileSystems** — tagline: `KidBus — school transport, completely visible.`
- A single WhatsApp broadcast list of school owners/principals (your highest-converting channel).

**Formats available per film:** square master (`videos/`), vertical cut for Reels/Stories/Shorts
(`videos/9x16/`), landscape cut for LinkedIn desktop/YouTube/website (`videos/16x9/`). Identical
audio and identical text placement in all three — pick by where you are posting, never re-edit.

**Golden rules for this product's social:**

1. Lead with the **parent's fear or the principal's pain**, never with features.
2. Always end with **one** call to action: *"DM 'PILOT' for a free 30-day pilot."*
3. Reply to every comment within an hour with a question back — school owners buy from conversations.
4. Post the **same file** to Instagram and LinkedIn but change the first line (parents vs. buyers).

## Measurement (what to actually watch)

| Metric | Where | What "good" looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-second hold rate | Instagram Insights | > 55 % on the 15 s films |
| Average watch time | Instagram / LinkedIn | > 60 % of the film length |
| Profile → link clicks | LinkedIn page analytics | 3–8 % of viewers |
| DMs with the word PILOT | Instagram / WhatsApp | the only metric that pays |
| Cost per pilot signup | Ads manager | under ₹400 for a school owner lead |

If the 3-second hold rate is below 40 %, the problem is almost always the **first frame** — replace it
with the thumbnail from `social/thumbs/<video>_1.jpg` (all of them are built to be readable at grid
size).
